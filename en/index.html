03 — FARM INFRASTRUCTURE

Infrastructure is yield.

A farm that works all year needs water, machines, stores and decent housing for the people who run it.

  • Reservoirs & automatic irrigation

    Reservoirs hold rainy-season water; sprinklers for pasture, drip for orchards, timed by soil moisture.

  • Machinery station

    Tractors, planters and harvesters; a maintenance workshop and a schedule moving machines between clusters.

  • Inputs, fertiliser & feed store

    Dry, palletised stores; first in, first out, batch number on every bag.

  • Veterinary & crop-protection store

    Separate locked, ventilated store with a stock register; issued only by trained staff.

  • Biogas & waste treatment

    Digesters, settling ponds and compost sheds, downwind and away from water sources.

  • Housing for workers & engineers

    Housing away from production, with kitchen, showers, clean water and rest areas.

05

05 — SUSTAINABILITY

Soil, water and local people.

  1. 01

    Keeping soil

    Contour farming on slopes, mulching with banana leaves and residues, grass–crop rotation.

  2. 02

    Keeping water

    Rainwater reservoirs, drip irrigation timed by sensors; livestock effluent never reaches streams.

  3. 03

    Local jobs

    Hiring first from villages around the farms, with skills and safety training in Lao.

  4. 04

    Smallholder links

    Technical support and guaranteed offtake for banana and grass growers in the sourcing area.
